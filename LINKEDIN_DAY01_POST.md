**Are we solving a performance problem—or simply delivering another training programme?**

A business leader notices delays, customer complaints, and inconsistent results. The immediate request to L&D: “We need training.”

But what if employees already understand the job—and unclear decision authority, fragmented information, or slow systems are preventing them from performing?

That changes the question.

Instead of asking **“What training should we deliver?”**, we should ask **“What prevents the required performance, and what evidence supports the cause?”**

My approach to Training Needs Analysis starts with five decisions:

1. Define the business performance gap.
2. Validate root causes using evidence.
3. Separate capability gaps from process, system, and incentive barriers.
4. Compare learning and non-learning interventions by impact, cost, and risk.
5. Pilot, measure, and scale what works.
