The operational objective in brief

This framework establishes a clear "Co-Creation" model. The L&D team is responsible for visual storytelling, layout optimisation, brand compliance, and visual design alignment. The stakeholder remains the **Subject Matter Expert (SME)** and must provide the foundation. By structuring our pipeline into Tiered SLAs, we eliminate the friction of tight timelines and loose, unstructured content.

Stakeholder Action: Submit form and attach core draft outline at least 15 business days before required launch date. Please clickhereto access the form.

Requirements for successful submission:

  • A clearly defined target audience and training context.
  • A complete text-outline draft, rough storyboard, or Word document detailing exactly what content belongs on each slide.
  • Brand compliance parameters (Exol University Brand Book or custom sub-brand assets).
  • Any high-resolution charts, screenshots, or proprietary diagrams needed.
Mandatory Rule: Incomplete submissions cannot be scheduled. The "No Blank Page" rule protects resources and ensures timelines are grounded in content.
Step 2
Triage, Scoping, and Scheduling
The L&D Project Manager assesses requirements, assigns a Tier, and checks resource bandwidth.
★
PM Action: The triage and scoping phase is completed within 2 business days of submission.

Intake Review Parameters:

  • Check attached assets for completeness (return to stakeholder immediately if lacking critical text outlines).
  • Assign the request to **Tier 1 (Polish)**, **Tier 2 (Redesign)**, or **Tier 3 (Conception)** based on content maturity.
  • Review the real-time Team Bandwidth Tracker to assign the project to the Lead Designer on weekly rotation.
Why it matters: Structured triage prevents bottlenecking, flags timeline conflicts early, and gives stakeholders realistic delivery expectations based on live capacity.
Step 3
Kickoff Call & Alignment Meeting
Brief 15-minute briefing session for complex decks to establish design goals and content priorities.
★
Alignment Action: Recommended for Tier 2 projects; mandatory for Tier 3 projects before design begins.

Meeting Objectives:

  • Align on learning objectives and visual flow.
  • Discuss any visual styling guidelines, preferred templates, and image specifications.
  • Confirm key milestones and lock down the target delivery date under agreed SLA guidelines.
Outcome: Eliminates misaligned drafts and reduces revision times by addressing visual direction before a single slide is built.
Step 4
Production Design & Stakeholder Review
Developing the presentation materials with strict feedback iteration limits.
★
Design Protocol: We use a "First Look" concept layout before producing the entire deck.

Execution and Review Flow:

  • The assigned designer generates a "First Look" (Concept + first 3 slides) to secure color, font, and layout alignment.
  • Following concept sign-off, the full presentation deck is built and delivered.
  • The stakeholder reviews and compiles all edits. **A maximum of two formal rounds of revisions are permitted.**
Discipline Clause: Consolidated, prompt feedback within 2 business days is required to keep the project on its SLA timeline. Late reviews push delivery day-for-day.
Step 5
Final Delivery & CSAT Evaluation
Completing handover and logging project performance metrics.
★
Closure Action: Deliver editable source file and log feedback.

Handover Process:

  • L&D delivers the final optimised presentation deck in editable (PPTX / Keynote) format.
  • The stakeholder is sent a 1-minute automated feedback survey. Please click here to access the form.
  • Operational stats (adherence to SLA, revision count) are automatically pushed to the team's quarterly KPI dashboard.
Continuous Improvement: The stakeholder satisfaction (CSAT) score ensures our design standard remains elite and the process stays highly valued.
02 · Stakeholder Expectations

The "Co-Creation" Model Boundaries.

Setting firm boundaries protects curriculum deliverables and guarantees high-quality visual outputs. These standard rule sets apply to all stakeholders.

Rule 01 — The "No Blank Page" Policy

Our visual designers and instructional developers are not Subject Matter Experts (SMEs). We cannot write training copy or storyboard messages from scratch.

Stakeholder Action: A structured text outline, rough draft, or detailed copy must be completed and approved before submitting the intake request.
Rule 02 — Clear Learning Objectives

Every deck designed by the L&D team must have a validated training purpose. Visuals must reinforce learning objectives, not just decorate slides.

Stakeholder Action: Clearly state on the intake form what target audience behaviors or knowledge gaps the training deck is built to address.
Rule 03 — Strict SLA Discipline

We operate with a lean 3-member team. Late feedback reviews, last-minute copy rewrites, or late-stage scoping adjustments break our pipeline.

Stakeholder Action: Compile consolidated stakeholder feedback and return it to the L&D team within 2 business working days of receiving drafts.
Rule 04 — Curricular Safeguards

Our team's primary mandate remains building end-to-end curriculum materials. Presentation slide design must be balanced to prevent curriculum delays.

Stakeholder Action: Plan requests well in advance of training launches. "Rush" jobs (<48 hrs) require written Global Head of L&D(designated leadership) sign-off.
03 · Service Level Agreements

Realistic Turnaround Times & Capacity Tiers.

Requests are placed into three distinct operational tiers based on the maturity of provided copy, slide count, and visual complexity.

Tier 1 Basic Polish
Formatting & Brand Alignment

Polishing already-completed presentation slides. Fixing fonts, alignment, color compliance, basic layout adjustments, and basic icon addition (up to 15 slides).

Turnaround & Scope

5 Business Days SLA. Unlimited monthly capacity, requiring complete content readiness prior to project queue start.

Tier 2 Standard Redesign
Visual Layout & Enhancements

Transforming rough drafts/outlines into structured, visual slides. Includes customised layout design, diagrams, icon systems, and hierarchy adjustments (up to 20 slides).

Turnaround & Scope

10 Business Days SLA. Hard maximum of 4 decks per month across the 3-member team to protect primary curriculum development.

Tier 3 Complex Build
Creative Storyboarding & Design

Co-creating training slides from raw outlines, unformatted bullet lists, or documents. Narrative styling, custom layouts, custom illustration suggestions, and infographics (up to 30 slides).

Turnaround & Scope

15 Business Days SLA. Hard maximum of 1–2 decks per month across the team. Requires early alignment and Project Manager booking.
