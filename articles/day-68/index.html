03 / Delivery alternatives

Three ways to respond to the constraint.

Option A

Accelerate delivery

Compress schedules and focus on risk analysis.

Risk: Defects and rework may outweigh speed.

Option B

Redesign workflow

Fix escalation process and risk visibility.

Risk: Requires coordination and may delay launch.

Option C · Controlled plan

Evidence-gated delivery

Use likelihood, impact, early-warning triggers, mitigation owners and escalation gates. Prioritize high-risk work packages.

Risk: Sponsors must accept transparent trade-offs.
